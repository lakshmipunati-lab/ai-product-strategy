## Portability Score
**Locked** — Fully coupled to Microsoft ecosystem. Microsoft Copilot 
is already active under the enterprise agreement, meaning vendor 
lock-in is not a future risk — it is the current state. Windsurf 
adds a secondary dependency with no fallback. No abstraction layer, 
no routing failover, and no eval framework exist. A full vendor 
swap estimated at 6-8 weeks minimum disruption. A Microsoft 
Copilot mandate or capability change requires zero new procurement 
— it can happen overnight.

---

## If Microsoft doubles Copilot pricing tomorrow:
Unlike a third-party vendor relationship, Microsoft Copilot pricing 
is embedded in BlackRock's enterprise M365 agreement. A price 
increase affects the entire organization simultaneously — not 
just engineering teams. No individual team can opt out without 
an enterprise-level contract renegotiation.

48-hour response: escalate immediately to IT and procurement as 
a contract issue, not a tooling issue. Activate manual fallback 
workflows. Begin fast-track compliance evaluation of one 
pre-approved alternative — Windsurf enterprise or an 
open-source coding assistant — that can be deployed without 
a new enterprise agreement.

---

## If Microsoft expands Copilot to include native QA test generation:
This is the highest-risk scenario and requires no new procurement 
decision on BlackRock's part — Microsoft simply ships the feature 
inside existing M365 licenses. Timeline: could happen in any 
quarterly Microsoft product update. No warning required.

What's defensible: ReleaseIQ's BlackRock-specific compliance 
context — wash-sale rule logic, TLH test patterns, SEC/FINRA 
audit trail requirements, and DI-specific edge cases — cannot 
be replicated by a generic Microsoft Copilot feature without 
years of institutional training data. A native Copilot test 
generator will produce generic output that does not meet 
BlackRock DI compliance standards out of the box.

The defense is formalizing ReleaseIQ as the compliance-approved 
QA standard before Microsoft ships this feature. Once ReleaseIQ 
is embedded in the SDLC with documented compliance credentials, 
replacing it requires a compliance review — not just an IT 
directive. That review process is your moat. The window to 
establish this is 3-6 months at most.
